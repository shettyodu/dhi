Asset Data Group · Data handling

Your data, handled safely.
And proven, every step.

Once we have an agreement, we prepare your data ourselves and never hand it to an outside company. We keep a clear record of everything we do to it, so you can see exactly what happened.

Why this matters

A typical broker hands your data to some other company to get it ready, and can't really tell you what happens to it after that. We do the work ourselves, start to finish, and we keep a record that shows what we did to every record, proves what we took out, and confirms what we did not keep.

How we handle your data

Eight steps, from the moment we have an agreement. You approve the key ones, and every step is logged.

1

We agree what's included, and start small

Encrypted · named access · logged

We write down exactly what's covered and start with a small sample, not your whole dataset.

2

We write a plan, and you approve it

You approve before any full run

We find where personal information is and show you a plan for what gets removed or hidden.

3

We remove the personal details

No original kept · no traceable key

We clean the data where it lives, so only a de-identified copy leaves, with IDs swapped for codes that can't be traced back.

4

A person double-checks it

Two sign-offs before any buyer

A real person reviews the cleaned data and looks for anything that could still point to someone.

5

Buyers work in a locked room, not with a copy

Access named · timed · logged

Buyers use the data inside a monitored clean room and can't walk off with a bulk copy.

6

You get a full record of what we did

Your proof it was handled right

A report shows what we did to every field, who opened the data, and what we didn't keep.

7

We keep as little as possible, then delete it

Certificate of deletion

We delete the detailed bits on schedule and return or certify deletion of your source.

8

You stay in charge

Nothing outside what you approve

You own it the whole time, approve every buyer, and buyers can't re-identify anyone or pass it on.

An honest note: removing personal details lowers the chance someone could be identified again, but no method can promise that is impossible. For sensitive data such as health records, a qualified expert reviews and signs off first.

Why this beats a typical broker

A typical broker introduces a buyer and then hands your data to some other company to prepare it. They can't tell you who touched it or what became of it. We keep the whole thing in house, one team start to finish, and we write it all down. We can show you what we did to every record, prove what we removed, and confirm what we did not keep. A broker that only makes introductions can't give you that.

What you receive

  • ✓ A cleanup plan you approved.
  • ✓ Your cleaned dataset, prepared to that plan.
  • ✓ A full report: where the data came from, what we did to it, and who opened it.
  • ✓ A certificate showing what we did not keep, and that we deleted the source where that applies.
