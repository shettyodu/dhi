Your data, handled safely.
And proven, every step.

Asset Data Group prepares your data itself, under one controlled chain of custody, and gives you an audit trail that shows what was done to every record, proves what was removed, and certifies what was not kept.

The process, step by step

1

Scope and secure intake

We start from the agreement. On the Order Form we fix exactly which records are in scope: the systems, record types, date range, fields, and anything excluded. You then provide a small, representative sample rather than your full dataset, so we can confirm the structure and build the plan without moving more data than necessary. Where data must move, it moves once, over a single encrypted channel, to a named location that only authorized Asset Data Group personnel can reach, and every access is logged.

2

De-identification plan, reviewed by you

We inspect the sample: the field names, the data types, where personal or regulated information appears, and any free-text fields. From that we write a plan that states, field by field, what will be removed, masked, tokenized, or generalized, and how free text will be scrubbed. You review and approve that plan before any full run, and nothing is marketed from the sample.

3

De-identification, at the source

The de-identification runs where your data lives, inside your environment or a controlled Asset Data Group environment, using our own tooling. Only the cleaned output leaves. Direct identifiers are removed, masked, or replaced with one-way tokens; dates are generalized; geography is reduced; and names, contacts, and identifiers are scrubbed out of free text. Where records need to stay linked, we use one-way tokens, so the data still connects without being identifiable.

What we do not claim: de-identification reduces the risk of re-identification, but it is not, by itself, a guarantee that re-identification is impossible. Where the data or the method requires it, for example health data, a qualified expert reviews and signs off under the Expert Determination standard.

4

Human quality check and risk review

Software is the first pass, not the last word. A person reviews a documented sample of the cleaned output to confirm that no personal information slipped through, with particular attention to free text, and checks the re-identification risk from rare combinations of fields such as dates, geography, and uncommon codes. Two sign-offs, one technical and one compliance, are required before anything is shown to a buyer.

5

Clean room and controlled access

Your prepared data is held and evaluated in a clean room: a controlled, access-logged environment. A buyer can work with the data inside that environment and see results, rather than walking away with a raw bulk copy. Access is granted to named people, is time limited, and is logged. Delivery, when it happens, follows the method agreed in your Order Form, whether that is a controlled transfer, a clean-room session, or access within your own environment.

6

Audit trail and attestation

Every step produces a record. The audit report documents what was in scope, a fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) of the source file, the exact treatment applied to each field, the number of items redacted from free text, who accessed the data and when, the quality sign-offs, and a certification of what was not retained. This is your evidence that your data was handled exactly as agreed.

7

Minimum hold, return, and certified deletion

Asset Data Group does not keep your identified source data, and holds no key, code list, or crosswalk that could re-identify the cleaned data. Identifier-level detail is deleted within the window set in your agreement, and any copy of your source data is returned or certified as deleted on schedule, with the certificate filed. If we ever receive a legal request for your data, we notify you where lawful and disclose only what the law requires.

8

Ongoing control

You keep ownership throughout and approve every buyer and every use. Every downstream buyer agreement bars re-identification and redistribution and limits use to what was agreed. Nothing is marketed, licensed, or shared outside the scope you approved.

Why this is different from a broker

Traditional brokers often introduce a buyer and then hand your account, and sometimes your data, to a third party to prepare it. They cannot tell you who touched it or what became of it. Asset Data Group keeps the entire process in house, under one chain of custody, and documents it. We can show you what was done to every record, prove what was removed, and certify what was not kept. That is a level of assurance an introduction-only broker cannot offer.
