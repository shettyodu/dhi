Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Asset Data Group ("ADG," "we," "us") is a data asset commercialization firm. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little as possible and to keep you in control.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide. When you use our forms, we collect your name, work email, organization, and the descriptions of your operational data or interests that you choose to submit. The assessment never asks you to upload an actual dataset.
  • Limited technical information. Standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) used to operate and secure the site.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss whether your organization may have a marketable data asset.
  • To produce the preliminary, non-binding assessment you request.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the website.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Your data, and data you later engage us to prepare

Assessments on this site use only the description you provide. If you later choose to proceed, personal and regulated information is de-identified or excluded and legal review occurs before any data is prepared, marketed, or licensed, and nothing moves without your written approval. You retain ownership of your data throughout.

Retention and deletion

We keep your submission and contact details only to follow up with you. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by emailing contact@assetdatagrp.com.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the site and communicate with you (for example, hosting and email), under confidentiality obligations, and where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Security

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected with access controls. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Cookies and local storage

This site uses only essential browser storage needed for its features (for example, remembering a referral code). It does not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact@assetdatagrp.com.

This policy is a general description of our practices and is not legal advice.
